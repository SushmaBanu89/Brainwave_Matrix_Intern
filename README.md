# Brainwave_Matrix_Intern

✅ Problem Statement
Retail businesses often struggle with making timely decisions due to a lack of clear visibility into key performance metrics like sales, profit, and customer behavior. The absence of dynamic, real-time analytics makes it difficult to identify high-performing categories, optimize inventory, and understand customer spending patterns across states and time periods.

💡 Solution Overview
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard that provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance, quantity sold, and profit. The dashboard enables decision-makers to filter insights by state, year, and month, while using visually engaging charts to analyze product sub-categories, customer behavior, and promotional impacts on profitability.

🛠️ What I Did
Created calculated KPIs for Total Sales, Total Quantity, and Profit.

Implemented dynamic filters for State, Year, and Month to enable drill-down analysis.

Built interactive visuals, including:

Pie Chart for Sales by Sub-category

Donut Chart for Profit by Discounted Product Name

Gauge for Quantity by Sum of Sales

Column Chart for Customer vs Total Sales

Column Chart for Sub-category vs Sum of Sales

Ensured clean data modeling and relationships for accurate analysis.
